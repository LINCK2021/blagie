Signed Contract Required

No work begins until a contract is signed. The contract defines the project scope, terms, and hourly rate of $40/hour.

Hourly Billing ($40/hour)

All work is billed at $40/hour. Billable time includes, but is not limited to, client communication, project discussions, research, negotiations, design development, file preparation, revisions, exports, uploads, and file delivery. A detailed timesheet is maintained for all projects.

Estimates Are Not Fixed Quotes

Project estimates are provided for planning purposes only and do not constitute fixed-price quotes. Final billing is based on actual hours worked at the agreed hourly rate.

50% Non-Refundable Deposit

A non-refundable deposit equal to 50% of the initial project estimate is required before work begins. The deposit reserves project availability and applies only to the project specified in the contract. Deposits are not transferable to other projects, services, or future work.

Electronic Payments Only

Payments are accepted exclusively through bank transfer or approved electronic payment platforms. Cash payments and informal payment arrangements are not accepted.

Original Design Work Only

Services are limited to original brand identity design. Requests to edit, replicate, adapt, complete, or modify work created by other designers are not accepted. Social media template design is not offered.

Client Response Deadline

Client feedback, approvals, requested content, and project materials must be provided within 3 business days. Delayed responses automatically place the project on hold.

Project Closure Due to Inactivity

If the client is inactive for more than 7 consecutive calendar days, the project is considered closed. Any continuation of work requires a new contract and a new 50% non-refundable deposit.

Release of Deliverables

Work files, deliverables, and usage rights are released only after full payment has been received.
